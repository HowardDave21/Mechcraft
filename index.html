Account data, in plain terms

What we collect

An account here is a username and a password, and nothing else. There is no email address, no date of birth, no real name, and no contact details asked for or stored at any point.

Where it lives

Your account and your save are kept only in this browser's local storage, on this device. There is no remote server behind this game, so nothing is transmitted anywhere, and nothing is visible to anyone else, including us.

Passwords

Your password is never stored as plain text. It is salted and hashed on your own device before it is saved, so the password itself is never recoverable, even from the stored data.

Limits worth knowing

Because everything lives in this browser, there is no password recovery, no cross device sync, and clearing your browser's site data will remove your account and save permanently.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from the Account control once you are signed in. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
